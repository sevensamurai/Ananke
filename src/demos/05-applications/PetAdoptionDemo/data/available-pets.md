# Our Available Pets

We currently have a wonderful group of animals looking for their forever homes. Each one has been health-checked, vaccinated, spayed or neutered, and microchipped. Here's a bit about each of them.

---

Meet Buddy. He's a one-year-old golden retriever with more energy than he knows what to do with. Buddy loves fetch, swimming, and running alongside bicycles. He knows sit, stay, and shake, but loose-leash walking is still a work in progress. He'd do best with an active family that has a fenced yard. Buddy gets along great with other dogs but can be a bit too enthusiastic for cats. Adoption fee: $100.

Luna is a three-year-old domestic shorthair tabby cat. She's the queen of window perches and will spend hours watching birds outside. Luna is independent but affectionate on her own terms — she'll curl up in your lap during movie nights but doesn't love being picked up. She's fine as an only pet and does well in apartments. Luna is FIV-positive, which sounds scary but just means she should stay indoors (which she prefers anyway) and may need more frequent vet checkups. Adoption fee: $75.

Then there's Max, our gentle senior. Max is an eight-year-old beagle mix who came to us when his owner moved into assisted living. He's calm, fully house-trained, and an absolute couch potato. Max is great with kids of all ages and ignores cats entirely. He takes daily joint supplements (we'll send him home with a supply) and prefers short walks over long hikes. If you want a low-maintenance companion who's already past the chewing-everything phase, Max is your guy. Adoption fee: $50.

Cleo is a two-year-old black cat with the most expressive green eyes you've ever seen. She was found as a stray and took a while to warm up, but now she's a total love bug. Cleo does best in a quiet home without young children — she startles easily at loud noises. She's bonded with another shelter cat, Mochi, and we'd love to adopt them together, but it's not required. Adoption fee: $75.

Mochi is Cleo's best friend — a one-year-old orange-and-white domestic longhair. Unlike Cleo, Mochi is fearless. He greets strangers at the door, plays fetch with crinkle balls, and has been known to ride around on people's shoulders. Mochi would thrive in a busy household. He needs regular brushing (that long fur mats easily) but is otherwise very low-maintenance. Adoption fee: $120.

Rocky is a three-year-old pit bull terrier mix and one of the most misunderstood dogs in the shelter. He's gentle, goofy, and desperate to be someone's best friend. Rocky passed his canine behavior assessment with flying colors and loves learning new tricks — he recently mastered "roll over" and "high five." He needs to be the only pet in the home, as he can be dog-selective, but he's great with adults and older kids (10+). Adoption fee: $100.

Daisy is a five-year-old Holland Lop rabbit. She's litter-trained, loves being petted (especially behind the ears), and does adorable binkies when she's happy. Daisy needs supervised free-roam time daily — she'll chew baseboards and cables if left unsupervised, so bunny-proofing is a must. She's been living in a foster home with a cat and they coexist peacefully. Adoption fee: $40.

Captain Flint is a seven-year-old African Grey parrot. He's incredibly intelligent and has a vocabulary of about 50 words and phrases, including "good morning," "want a treat," and an impressively accurate microwave beep. Captain Flint needs an experienced bird owner — he can be nippy with new people and requires several hours of daily interaction and mental stimulation. His cage needs to be large (minimum 36" × 24" × 48"). He bonds strongly with one person but tolerates others. Adoption fee: $60.

Pepper is a four-year-old border collie mix. She's wicked smart and needs a job to do — without mental stimulation, she'll create her own entertainment (think: rearranging your shoes). Pepper excels at agility and knows about 20 commands. She's selective about dog friends but fine with cats. Ideal home: someone who runs, hikes, or does dog sports. Adoption fee: $100.

Whiskers is a six-year-old orange tabby who weighs a robust 16 pounds. Our vet says he needs to lose about 3 pounds, so his adopter should be committed to portion control and interactive play. Despite his size, Whiskers is surprisingly agile and loves chasing feather toys. He's easygoing, gets along with dogs and cats, and purrs like a diesel engine. Adoption fee: $75.

Last but not least, there's Ziggy, a two-year-old chihuahua-dachshund mix (a "chiweenie"). Ziggy is five pounds of pure attitude. He loves burrowing under blankets, barking at the mailman, and sitting on the highest spot he can find. Ziggy does best as the only pet and prefers adults or homes with older children. He's fully house-trained and his small size makes him perfect for apartments. Despite the tough-guy act, he's a total cuddler once he trusts you. Adoption fee: $100.
