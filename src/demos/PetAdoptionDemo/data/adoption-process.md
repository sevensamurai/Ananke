# Pet Adoption Process

## Who Can Adopt?

Anyone 21 years or older with a valid photo ID can apply. We welcome all living situations — houses, apartments, condos — as long as the pet will be safe and loved. If you're renting, we'll need written landlord approval confirming pets are allowed.

## How It Works

Our adoption process is designed to find the perfect match between you and your new companion. Here's what to expect:

### Step 1: Browse & Meet

Visit our shelter during open hours (Tuesday through Sunday, 10am–5pm) or browse available pets on our website. Our staff can help you find animals that match your lifestyle, living situation, and experience level.

### Step 2: Submit an Application

Fill out our adoption application form — it takes about 15 minutes. We ask about your home environment, daily schedule, other pets, and experience with animals. There are no wrong answers; this helps us make sure the match will work for both of you.

### Step 3: Meet & Greet

Once your application is reviewed (usually within 24–48 hours), we'll schedule a meet-and-greet with the pet you're interested in. For dogs, we recommend bringing all household members, including other dogs, for a supervised introduction.

### Step 4: Home Check

For certain breeds or animals with special needs, we may schedule a brief home visit. This is not an inspection — it's to help us give you tailored advice on preparing your space (fence height, safe rooms for cats, etc.).

### Step 5: Finalize & Go Home

Sign the adoption agreement, pay the adoption fee, and take your new family member home! Every adoption includes a starter kit with food samples, a toy, and our care guide.

## Adoption Fees

Fees cover spaying/neutering, vaccinations, microchipping, and a basic health exam:

- Puppies (under 1 year): $150
- Adult dogs (1–7 years): $100
- Senior dogs (8+ years): $50
- Kittens (under 1 year): $120
- Adult cats (1–7 years): $75
- Senior cats (8+ years): $50
- Rabbits: $40
- Birds: $60

## Timeline

Most adoptions are completed within 1–2 weeks from initial application. Same-day adoptions are possible for adult cats and small animals when the application is straightforward.

## What to Bring on Adoption Day

- A carrier or crate appropriate for your pet's size
- A leash and collar for dogs
- Valid photo ID
- Payment for the adoption fee (card or cash)
- Patience and excitement!

## Post-Adoption Support

We don't disappear after the adoption. Every adopter gets:

- A free vet checkup within 14 days at our partner clinic
- Access to our behavior helpline for the first 60 days
- Discounted training classes for dogs
- A welcome email with tailored tips for your specific pet

If things aren't working out, we have a 30-day return policy — no questions asked. We'd rather the pet come back to us than end up in a bad situation.
